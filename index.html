Who we are

MadarTech iVMS operates the MadarTech Rental mobile app and the OmRents website at https://omrents.pro/. Our business address is 18 November Street, behind Azaiba Mosque, Muscat 112, Oman. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean MadarTech iVMS.

MadarTech Rental displays OmRents in an embedded browser. The app is published on Google Play under the developer display name Mithqal LLC.

This policy covers information handled through both MadarTech Rental and OmRents, including website accounts, rental services, and records submitted through the website.

Information you provide through OmRents

When you sign in, complete a website form, or upload an attachment, the information is transmitted to the website or the service identified in that feature. The native app does not create a separate MadarTech Rental account or copy form submissions to a separate app-operated server.

Depending on the features you use, information entered through the website can include:

  • Account identifiers and sign-in credentials, including a username or PIN.
  • Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and other customer or business contact details entered in forms.
  • Vehicle details, rental requests, reservations, contract details, and related records entered or displayed in a workspace.
  • Images and documents you choose to upload. These can contain identity or passport information if you use the website’s customer-document features.

Some rental-service features require an account or particular details or documents; you can choose not to use those features. The service also handles the contact details and information you send when requesting support or exercising your privacy choices.

Customers do not enter online payment-card details or make online payments through OmRents. Payments take place outside the platform. Information supplied directly to a rental company or another payment service is handled by that recipient under its terms and privacy practices.

Do not send account passwords, PINs, or identity documents to our privacy email when making a general inquiry.

Connection information and local storage

Loading OmRents sends standard network information to the website and its resource providers, including your IP address and browser or device request information. This information is used to deliver and maintain the rental service and its operation and security.

The embedded browser can store website cookies, sign-in session information, cached content, and website storage on your device. This lets website sessions and preferences persist between app visits. The app does not automatically clear this information when you close or restart it.

OmRents does not use visitor analytics tools. The current native app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not send separate usage reports to an app-operated server.

Files and device permissions

On Android, the app uses the system file picker when you choose to attach a file. It receives access to the files you select rather than requesting broad access to your device’s storage. The website receives an attachment when its upload feature sends it.

For an iOS version using the current implementation, website attachment controls can offer access to photos or user-initiated camera capture. Camera, photo-library, or microphone access may be requested for the attachment feature you select; microphone access can be used for sound in a video attachment. Access is subject to the device’s permission controls.

The current native app has no feature that reads your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, advertising identifier, or precise location. It has no background recording feature. You can decline available device permissions or change them in system settings, although the corresponding attachment feature may then be unavailable.

How information is used

The app uses network access and embedded-browser storage to display the website, maintain its browser session, and enable the website features you choose. Selected files are made available to the website’s upload control at your request.

We and the relevant authorized rental company or workspace users use information to authenticate users, provide the requested car-rental service, manage vehicles and rental records, maintain the service's operation and security, and respond to support and privacy requests.

If you contact info@madargps.com, MadarTech iVMS receives the email address and information you include so we can respond to your request. Retention of correspondence is described below.

We use personal information for the rental service and its necessary operation. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Who receives information

Access to rental-service information is limited to MadarTech iVMS, the relevant authorized rental company or workspace users, and providers needed to operate the service. These providers support functions such as website hosting, storage, authentication, and communications. Information is made available to the parties involved in supplying the service you use.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request, or where necessary to address a legitimate security matter. We do not share information for advertising or sell it.

When you choose a phone, email, messaging, map, or external document link, the app can open the relevant installed application or external service and pass the selected link to it. If a link includes information supplied by the website, that information accompanies the link. Any information you subsequently provide to the external service is handled under its privacy practices.

Security

The app starts at the HTTPS version of OmRents. It does not bypass website certificate errors, and the Android version blocks unencrypted HTTP traffic and mixed content in its embedded browser. These controls protect the connection; they do not describe or guarantee the website’s server security.

Access to service records is limited to the authorized parties described above. Protect your account credentials and your device, and sign out when appropriate on a device shared with others. No transmission or storage system can provide an absolute guarantee against unauthorized access.

Retention

Website cookies, cached content, and local website storage remain on the device until they expire or are removed by the website or the device’s app-data controls. On Android, you can clear MadarTech Rental’s storage in system settings; this can sign you out and remove local website preferences. Clearing local storage or uninstalling the app does not delete records held on website servers.

We retain account and rental-service information while it is needed to provide the service and manage the relevant rental records. You can request deletion of your account and associated information as described below.

When we fulfill a deletion request, we delete the relevant account and associated personal information, except information that must remain for a legitimate legal or security reason. Any retained information is limited to what is needed for that reason and kept only for as long as that reason applies. We explain any applicable retention exception when responding to the request.

Privacy and support correspondence is kept while needed to address the request and retain any necessary record of its resolution, subject to applicable legal or security obligations.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can choose whether to sign in or provide information to website features. Some features require an account, required form fields, or documents. You can stop using the app, cancel a file selection, and manage available device permissions in system settings.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or deletion of your OmRents account, email info@madargps.com. For an account-deletion request, use the subject MadarTech Rental account deletion and provide the username or account email needed to identify your account. If the request concerns a rental-company workspace or a particular rental record, identify it so we can arrange the appropriate action.

MadarTech iVMS receives these requests and arranges the relevant action, including deletion. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting on an account or record. Do not include your password, PIN, or a copy of an identity document in an initial email. We will explain if further information is necessary. You do not need to keep the app installed to email a deletion request.

Deleting the app, signing out, or clearing local storage does not itself delete an OmRents account or its server-held information. Your rights and any exceptions depend on the law that applies to you.

Intended users

MadarTech Rental and OmRents are intended for car-rental customers and authorized business users. Rental eligibility and any age requirements are determined by the relevant rental company's service terms. If you have a privacy concern relating to a child's information in a rental record, contact info@madargps.com.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the app or website's data practices change and revise its effective date. Updated policies will be available on this page. Where a change requires additional notice or a choice under applicable law, we will provide it.
